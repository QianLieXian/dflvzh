DFCN English name pronunciation: traditional British RP target

Primary dictionary: Britfone 3.0.1, Jose Llarena, MIT license.
https://github.com/JoseLlarena/Britfone
Pinned revision: 1062be14adc96c358f2087ac5449d72130c7a6f4
britfone.csv is the unchanged britfone.main.3.0.1.csv; original README and license accompany it.
The source is RP/Standard Southern British, including some modern vowel symbols.
The DFCN adapter normalizes IPA symbols, weak short happy /i/ to traditional /ɪ/, non-rhotic
vowels, long BATH, and centering diphthongs. This does not turn modern vocabulary into Old English.

Supplemental British dictionary: English (UK) MFA dictionary v3.1.0,
Michael McAuliffe and Morgan Sonderegger (2024), CC BY 4.0.
https://mfa-models.readthedocs.io/en/latest/dictionary/English/English%20(UK)%20MFA%20dictionary%20v3_1_0.html
https://github.com/MontrealCorpusTools/mfa-models/releases/download/dictionary-english_uk_mfa-v3.1.0/english_uk_mfa.dict
Retained unchanged. Entries contain either spelling and phones, or an additional four numeric
columns (pronunciation probability and silence statistics/corrections); these are not phone symbols.
The DFCN adapter reduces phonetic allophones to a citation-style RP phone inventory.

MFA-LICENSE.txt is the original CC BY 4.0 license from the repository at
d6eff86a42c6a90b641e17dfdf7a16555b934483. The model evaluation fields are null;
no accuracy claim is made from them. DFCN uses its own read-only OpenFST-format decoder,
without installing or linking Phonetisaurus, Pynini, OpenFST or a speech synthesizer.

Complete British word pronunciations precede morphological resolution, supplied DF WORD
boundaries and UK model predictions. DF WORD/POS information selects homographs and supplies
only existing legal compound boundaries; it never reconstructs or reorders the English input.
Unknown words are not segmented by searching arbitrary dictionary substrings or guessed affixes.
MFA alternatives use a fixed priority: unreduced citation forms, source pronunciation probability,
then original source order. Phone counts and Chinese output lengths do not select a reading.
English generated-name fallback has no target number of syllables or Chinese characters.
Conventional complete transcriptions precede phone rendering. Campbell, Panorama and Snow Hill
use the explicit forms documented by the China Institute of Toponymy in its 2011 UNGEGN paper;
Hampton and field retain the original fallback plan's complete transcriptions.
All content components stay in source order, including middle words. In a text-only name with
other content, naming particles the/of may be omitted following Wan and Verspoor (1998);
a/an use the same article policy as an engineering extension. Other prepositions/conjunctions
are not removed wholesale, and explicit DF content-word POS slots are always retained.
An input containing only particles retains them instead of selecting one arbitrary final word.
Britfone's real primary/secondary stress and weak-vowel notation guide pronunciation mapping,
not syllable selection. The stressless MFA dictionary/model do not fabricate stress from vowel
length, quality or position. Unmarked STRUT /ʌ,ɐ/ remains a full vowel.
Exact unknown compounds may expose native WORD roots for pronunciation; known whole words
retain dictionary priority. Word-initial consonant groups and consecutive syllables are not
cut down to a nearest consonant or a few isolated vowel anchors.
Compact output comes from complete CV/nasal combinations, weak-vowel+coda forms and familiar
group approximations such as final ts/dz and kw/gw/ju. Weak dən and tən remain distinct 登/顿.
Actual coda m before p/b may use the preceding syllable's n rhyme while preserving the following
stop; an onset m or other coda m retains its original mapping. This limited adaptation follows
the environment reported by Huang and Lin (2016), not a rule to remove all m sounds.
https://journals.linguisticsociety.org/proceedings/index.php/amphonology/article/view/3686
No generic truncation of weak syllables or guessed derivational roots is performed. Without an
attested natural short form, a more complete approximation is retained. This does not require
lossless sound preservation, promise that every name will be short, or claim naturalness for
every model prediction. No already-rendered Hanzi string is clipped to a character limit.
This pronunciation/transcription policy applies only to confirmed English source fields. Native DF
language spellings retain their existing independent processing and transcription tables;
Latin letters alone are not evidence that a generated name is English.
